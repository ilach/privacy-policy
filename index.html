Privacy Policy for MClock

Effective Date: November 15, 2026

LACHI ("we," "our," or "us") built the MClock application as a Commercial app. This page is used to inform visitors regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information if anyone decided to use our Service.

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (PII) from you while you use our application. You can use the app without creating an account or providing personal details such as your name, email address, or phone number.

2. Location Information

To provide accurate, real-time local weather forecasts, MClock requests access to your device’s current location (GPS or network-based location).

  • How it is used: Your location coordinates are used strictly in real-time to fetch weather data for your current area.
  • No Storage or Tracking: We do not store your location history on our servers, nor do we track your movements in the background or share your location data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

3. Third-Party Services (Weather Data)

To display weather forecasts and current conditions, the app connects to external weather data services provided by The Weather Company / Weather.com.

  • When the app requests weather information, your current location coordinates and standard technical request data (such as your IP address) are transmitted securely to Weather.com solely to retrieve the relevant forecast.
  • Please note that third-party services have their own privacy policies governing how they handle data. You can review the privacy policy of The Weather Company (Weather.com) on their official website.

4. Log Data and Analytics

We do not collect personal log data or use invasive third-party tracking SDKs. However, standard crash reports or basic technical diagnostics provided anonymously by the Google Play Store may be used solely to improve app stability and fix bugs.

5. Children’s Privacy

Our application does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Because no personal data is collected or stored, the app is safe for users of all ages.

6. Security

We value your trust in using our app. All network communications between the app and the weather data provider are encrypted using standard security protocols (HTTPS/TLS) to protect the data in transit.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at: igor@compower.pl.
